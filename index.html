80 years of wonder
A birthday journey · 1946–2026

Eighty years.
One remarkable life.

A celebration of the places you've been, the things you've loved, the adventures you've made—and the horizons still ahead.

Happy 80th birthday, Dad.

Chapter one · The great leap

Some journeys change everything.

Prague. 1968. A young man leaves behind everything familiar, travels through Britain, and makes his way to Canada with little money, limited English, and no guarantee of what comes next.

What he does have is courage, curiosity, and the determination to make a life.

Chapter two · No ordinary beginning

A life built on possibility.

Hitchhiking across Canada. Working in the mines of northern Manitoba. Driving a taxi in Montreal. Finding his way into photography.

Not exactly a conventional résumé. Then again, convention was never really your style.

Every detour became part of the adventure.

Chapter three · A way of seeing

Always looking closer.

Photographs, Super 8 movies, cameras and lenses. From film to digital, there was always another idea, another technology, another way to capture the world.

And as an art teacher, you shared that curiosity and creativity with others.

You gave our family a remarkable collection of memories—quite literally.

Chapter four · The magical place

A chalet in the woods.

Snowy Easters. Long summer days. A fireplace, chopped wood, water pulled by hand from the well—and even a homemade meat smoker.

The cottage north of Montreal wasn't just a destination. It was a whole world of its own.

Some of our happiest memories began there.

Chapter five · A competitive spirit

Never one to sit out.

Hockey, soccer, tennis, table tennis, running, marathons—and, long before that, weightlifting.

Through Sokol and the Czech community, you helped keep a remarkable tradition alive: summers of friendship, competition, and belonging.

And yes, you always played to win.

Chapter six · Mountains and rivers

Always another horizon.

Hiking Mount Washington. Driving the Auto Road. Canoeing the Saco River. Snowy trails, summer roads, and plenty of reasons to head outdoors.

Those White Mountain adventures became a lasting part of our family's story—and the landscape I eventually chose to call home.

Chapter seven · On the road again

From snowbanks to palm trees.

Road trips to Sarasota. Adventures in New York City. A journey together to the top of the Twin Towers, back when they still stood.

And a return to Prague together after the Velvet Revolution—a remarkable circle completed.

The world was always worth seeing.

Chapter eight · An eye for the unusual

Great taste. According to you.

Picasso. Dalí. Henry Moore. Unconventional shapes, striking ideas, and beauty where others might not look.

Which may explain your appreciation for that most misunderstood masterpiece of automotive design: the Pontiac Aztek.

Fashion comes and goes. Independent judgment lasts forever.

Chapter nine · What's next?

Still looking up.

From classic cars to electric ones. From film cameras to digital marvels. From the dreams of supersonic flight to the possibility of people walking on Mars.

You've witnessed extraordinary changes—and you've never stopped wondering what might come next.

The future has always been one of your favorite subjects.

Chapter ten · The lasting adventure

What a life you've made.

You crossed borders, changed careers, explored the world, built a family, preserved a culture, and found new things to be passionate about again and again.

You taught us that obstacles are there to be met, and the unknown is something to explore.

There is so much to be proud of, Dad.

The next chapter · 80 and counting

Here's to what comes next.

At 80, there are still new inventions to marvel at, places to rediscover, stories to tell, and surprises waiting around the corner.

Happy birthday, Dad. With so much love—and admiration for the journey.

One more thing… 🎁

Your new iPad is on its way! It arrives in a couple of weeks. Consider it a new window onto everything that still sparks your curiosity.
